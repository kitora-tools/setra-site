SETRA

Privacy policy

Effective 9 November 2026.

Setra is a workout tracker. This policy says what Setra stores, where, why, and how you remove it. Setra has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and sells nothing about you.

On your phone

Everything you log (workouts, sets, routines, custom exercises, notes and preferences) is saved in the app's private storage on your phone first, so the app works without a connection. Your sign-in session is kept in the phone's secure storage (Android Keystore, iOS Keychain). Signing out or deleting your account removes your data from the phone; the app warns you first if changes have not been backed up yet. If you export a backup file, it goes only where you save or send it.

Setra asks for permission to show notifications only to tell you when a rest period ends.

Your data is sent only over encrypted connections and stored in the European Union: the database with Neon in Frankfurt, and backups on Google Cloud in Belgium. Accounts whose email is never verified are deleted after 7 days.

Who else handles it

Only the services that run Setra handle it: Google Cloud (hosting and backups), Neon (the database) and Google's Gmail (sending verification and password-reset emails, which needs your email address and the code). If you choose Google or Apple sign-in, their own privacy policies apply to that sign-in. We do not share your data with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Server logs, which never contain your passwords, codes or workout content, are kept 30 days. Database backups are kept up to 30 days, after which deleted data is gone from them too.

Deleting your data

In the app: open the Profile tab, tap your name, then Delete account. This removes your account and everything in it from our servers at once, and from your phone. No app? See delete your account.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data (the app's Export data gives you all of it), its correction or its deletion, or object to how it is used, by writing to setra.support.app@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days. If you are in the EU or UK, you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Setra is not meant for children under 16 and we do not knowingly hold their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app's release notes.
