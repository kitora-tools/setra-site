Delete your account and data

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Setra and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap your name, then Delete account.
  3. You can export a backup first. Confirm with your password, or with Google or Apple if you signed in with them.

Without the app

Email privacy@setra.app from the address of your account, with the subject "Delete my Setra account". We confirm it is you by replying to that address, then delete the account within 30 days and tell you when it is done.

What is deleted

Everything: your profile (username, email, display name), your password hash and linked Google or Apple sign-in, your sessions, and all your workouts, routines, custom exercises and preferences. Nothing is kept, except that encrypted database backups expire within 30 days.

Data still on a phone where you are signed in is removed when you delete in the app, or when you uninstall it.
